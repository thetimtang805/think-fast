# Western Daily Topics

50 English discussion prompts for the app's `English + Daily Topics` mode. They mirror the Chinese question bank's practical, debatable tone while using familiar Western social contexts.

## Family, Relationships, and Identity

1. Parents helping adult children buy a home: support or prolonged dependence?
2. Is moving away from your hometown still necessary to become independent?
3. Are modern weddings becoming social performances rather than personal celebrations?
4. Should couples combine their finances before they get married?
5. Is choosing not to have children becoming a practical decision rather than a personal preference?
6. Does therapy language help people set boundaries, or make ordinary conflict feel clinical?
7. Are friendship breakups harder than romantic breakups to talk about?
8. Is living alone a sign of freedom, or a symptom of social isolation?
9. Should adult children be expected to care for aging parents at home?
10. Are dating apps expanding choice, or making commitment harder?

18. Are side hustles empowering people, or normalizing unstable wages?
19. Does personal branding help people find opportunities, or force everyone to become a salesperson?
20. Is “follow your passion” still useful career advice?
