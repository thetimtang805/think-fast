# UX 面试题 · 逐字稿范本（中英双语）

为题库 50 道题，按各自推荐的回答框架（PREP / SPARK / RANGE）写的完整口语逐字稿，每题中文、English 各一份。定制思路与每步的开口句式见 `UX面试题-回答框架规则.md` 第 10 节。

## 怎么用

- **方括号 [ ] 是提示词**：里面是需要你换成**自己真实内容**的地方（项目、场景、做法、数字、当时的画面与感受、结果等）。「如：……」「e.g. …」是示例，可以直接用，也可以换。
- **方括号外面是固定话术**：通用的判断、方法和句式，可以直接念。
- **别背稿**：把方括号填成你的真实经历后，用自己的话顺一遍，保留结构和关键句即可。
- **五感加成**：每篇第 3 段里有一句带感官和情绪的方括号提示，换成你当时真实的画面和感受。
- **不要编造**：面试官会追问细节，方括号里只填你真的经历过的事。

## 1. HR / 招聘方：动机、经历与职业选择（1–8）

### 第 1 题　请用 3 分钟介绍一下你自己，以及最能代表你设计能力的经历。

*Could you introduce yourself and highlight a project that shows your design strengths?*

**框架：** PREP　**建议时长：** 180 秒

#### 中文

**P 观点**

我是 [你的设计方向和年限，如：有五年经验的 B 端产品设计师]，做过 [行业或产品类型，如：电商、金融工具、企业服务]。我最擅长把 [一类复杂或模糊的问题，如：流程冗长、信息混乱的业务场景] 变成 [用户能顺畅完成的体验，如：清晰、上手快的产品]，靠的核心能力是 [两三项能力，如：拆解问题、用户研究、跨团队推动落地]。

**R 理由**

第一，我长期处理的都是 [你反复处理的问题类型，如：多角色、多步骤的复杂流程]，所以形成了一个稳定的方法：先看清问题全貌，弄明白谁在什么情况下卡住了，再决定从哪里下手，而不是一上来就画界面。这样方案不容易做偏，团队也更容易对齐。第二，我更关心设计有没有真正带来变化，比如 [你关注的价值指标，如：完成率、效率、用户满意度]，所以每个决定我都会问自己：它能不能被验证？

**E 案例**

最能代表这个定位的是 [某个最贴合目标岗位的项目，如：订单流程改版]。当时 [项目背景与目标，如：业务希望提升下单完成率，但用户在中间几步大量流失]，我负责 [你的角色与范围，如：整体体验设计，并牵头用户研究]。最关键的一个决定是 [你的关键判断，如：没有急着改界面，而是先把流程拆开，找到真正卡住用户的一步]，因为与其同时改很多地方，不如先找到最关键的那一处，投入更小，也更容易看出改动有没有用。[我还记得当时会议室里投影亮着，看着用户测试的录像，大家都没出声，我手心微微出汗，但心里很想试一试新的方案]。最后我们 [你们做的调整]，结果 [结果，如：完成率提升了多少，或团队怎样评价]。

**P 回扣**

所以我想找的，是 [你想继续做的方向，如：有真实复杂度、能持续打磨体验的产品]，这个岗位正好 [你看到的匹配点，如：面对的是 XX 类问题，需要我的 XX 能力]。

#### English

**P · Point**

I'm [your design focus and years of experience, e.g. a B2B product designer with five years of experience], and I've worked in [industries or product types, e.g. e-commerce, fintech tools, enterprise software]. I'm best at turning [a kind of complex or fuzzy problem, e.g. long, confusing business workflows] into [an experience users can move through easily, e.g. something clear and quick to learn], mainly through [two or three core skills, e.g. breaking down problems, user research, driving alignment across teams].

**R · Reason**

First, most of my work involves [the type of problem you keep solving, e.g. multi-role, multi-step workflows], so I've built a steady habit: understand the whole problem, work out who gets stuck and when, and only then decide where to start, instead of opening the design tool right away. That keeps solutions from drifting and makes it easier for the team to align. Second, I care less about whether a design looks right and more about whether it changed something, such as [the value you track, e.g. completion rate, efficiency, user satisfaction], so for every decision I ask myself: can this be validated?

**E · Example**

The project that best represents this is [a project that fits the target role well, e.g. an order flow redesign]. The situation was [background and goal, e.g. the business wanted more completed orders, but users were dropping off in the middle steps], and I was responsible for [your role and scope, e.g. the end-to-end experience, and I led the user research]. The key call was [your key decision, e.g. not to touch the UI first, but to break the flow apart and find the one step that was really blocking people], because changing many things at once costs more and hides what actually worked. [I still remember the meeting room, the projector on, all of us watching a usability recording in silence. My palms were a little sweaty, but I really wanted to try the new approach.] In the end we [what you changed], and the result was [the outcome, e.g. the change in completion rate, or how the team responded].

**P · Point again**

That's why I'm looking for [the direction you want to keep working on, e.g. a product with real complexity where the experience can keep being refined], and this role fits because [the match you see, e.g. it deals with this type of problem and needs this strength of mine].

### 第 2 题　为什么选择 UX / 产品设计，而不是纯视觉设计或产品经理？

*Why did you choose UX or product design over visual design or product management?*

**框架：** PREP　**建议时长：** 90 秒

#### 中文

**P 观点**

我选择 UX，是因为我最享受用设计解决 [你最喜欢解决的问题类型，如：让复杂流程变简单、让用户少走弯路] 这类问题。我尊重视觉设计和产品经理这两个方向，只是相比 [视觉或 PM 的某个侧重，如：把界面打磨到极致，或统筹优先级与排期]，我更想待在从问题出发、为体验负责的位置上。

**R 理由**

原因有两点。第一是工作方式：UX 让我可以先拆解问题、理解用户，再决定怎么做，这种从为什么开始的思考方式，跟我的性格很合。第二是所处的位置：这个角色既贴近用户，又能和产品、研发、视觉、数据一起协作，我能把研究里看到的东西推到产品里去。

**E 案例**

让我确认这个选择的，是一次 [某个项目，如：注册流程优化]。当时我发现，真正的价值不在视觉，而在 [你的发现，如：用户不知道下一步会发生什么，他们缺的是清晰的预期]。[我还记得那天盯着测试用的屏幕，看到一个小小的提示文案被改掉，用户一下子顺畅地走完了流程，我忽然有点兴奋，心里一亮]。那一刻我意识到，[你的领悟，如：设计的价值可以是让一个决定变得容易]。

**P 回扣**

所以我会继续深耕 [你想深耕的方向，如：复杂业务流程与体验度量]，这也是我应聘这个岗位的原因，因为它 [岗位上你看重的一点，如：让设计从问题定义就参与进来]。

#### English

**P · Point**

I chose UX because what I enjoy most is solving [the kind of problem you like most, e.g. making a complicated flow simple, sparing users detours] through design. I respect visual design and product management, but compared with [what visual or PM work emphasizes, e.g. polishing an interface to perfection, or balancing priorities and schedules], I'd rather sit where the work starts from the problem and I'm accountable for the experience.

**R · Reason**

There are two reasons. First, the way I work: UX lets me break down the problem and understand users before deciding what to build, and starting from why suits how I think. Second, the position: the role stays close to users while working with product, engineering, visual design and data, so I can carry what I learn in research into the product.

**E · Example**

What confirmed it for me was [a project, e.g. a sign-up flow optimization]. I realized the real value wasn't in the visuals but in [your finding, e.g. users didn't know what would happen next, and what they lacked was a clear expectation]. [I still remember staring at the test screen when one small line of copy changed and the user glided through the flow. I felt a spark of excitement.] That's when I understood [your takeaway, e.g. that design can make a hard decision feel easy].

**P · Point again**

So I want to go deeper in [the direction you want to invest in, e.g. complex business workflows and measuring experience], which is also why I'm applying for this role: it [what you value about the role, e.g. brings design in from the moment the problem is defined].

比如在 [某个具体场景] 里，AI 给出了不太确定的结果。我用 [来源引用 / 置信提示 / 需要核对的标记] 呈现，并提供 [纠错入口和下一步建议，如：查看依据、改为手动、联系人工]。[测试时我听见用户小声嘀咕了一句，看到他盯着那句结果迟迟不点，我心里咯噔一下]，这让我进一步调整了 [你改动的地方]。结果 [结果，如：用户核对的比例、误用的减少]。

**P 回扣**

所以我把可信度当成产品责任来设计，让用户在 [关键决策时刻] 知道该信多少，也知道自己能做什么。这是他们敢用 AI 的前提，也是我在这个岗位上想守住的事。

#### English

**P · Point**

The interface should be honest about [where the AI is uncertain or may be biased], and always offer a next step. A one-line disclaimer that AI can make mistakes isn't enough, because users still don't know what to do afterward.

**R · Reason**

### 第 50 题　你如何设计 AI 产品的反馈闭环：收集哪些反馈、如何保护隐私、怎样用反馈提升体验？

*How would you collect AI feedback, protect privacy, and improve the experience?*

**框架：** RANGE　**建议时长：** 90 秒

#### 中文

**R 立场**

我设计 AI 产品反馈闭环的做法是：低打扰、有目的，并且尊重隐私。用户信任是前提，反馈收集如果让人觉得被监视或被打扰，用户会先离开。

**A 分几类**

我把反馈分成三类。一是显性反馈，也就是 [点赞、点踩、一键纠错]。二是隐性行为，也就是 [采纳、修改、重试、放弃这些行为信号]。三是深度反馈，也就是 [用户访谈、抽样评审]。

**N 挑一类讲透**

我讲隐性行为这一类，因为它量大，也最容易越界。我会先想清楚要 [回答的问题，如：用户是不是采纳了这次生成]，只收集 [必要的行为数据]，而不是用户的原始内容。需要看内容时，会 [先征得同意、做脱敏处理、说明用途]。[翻看用户留下的一条评论时，字里行间的无奈让我心头一软，也让我在收集这件事上更谨慎]。之后我把这些信号转成 [评估指标，如：采纳率、修改率、重试率]，找到 [问题集中的环节]，再 [据此改进设计]，并对比 [改进前后的变化]。

**G 升华**

不论哪一类反馈，我都坚持用户知情、可选，并且可以随时撤回。

**E 邀请**

关于反馈本身的偏差，比如 [只有极端体验的用户才会留言]，我也有一些思考，如果您感兴趣，我可以接着展开讲讲。

#### English

**R · Reply**

I design AI feedback loops to be low-interruption, purposeful, and respectful of privacy. User trust comes first, and if collecting feedback makes people feel watched or interrupted, they'll leave before they ever help us improve.

**A · Angles**

I group feedback into three types. One, explicit signals, like [thumbs up, thumbs down, one-tap corrections]. Two, implicit behavior, like [accepting, editing, retrying, or abandoning a result]. Three, deep feedback, like [user interviews and sampled reviews].
